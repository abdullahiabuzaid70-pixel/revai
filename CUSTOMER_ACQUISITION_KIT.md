# RevAI — First Pilot Customer Acquisition Kit

## 1. Who Is Your Target Customer

**Profile:**
- Mid-sized Nigerian companies (50-500 employees)
- Annual revenue: 200M - 2B NGN
- Industries: logistics, manufacturing, trade/import, healthcare, education
- Has a finance team (even if small) but NO dedicated forensic auditor
- Uses Excel, QuickBooks, or Sage for accounting — NOT fully automated
- Owner/CEO is involved in financial decisions (not delegating to a board)

**The person you're talking to:**
- Title: CFO, Finance Manager, or Managing Director/CEO
- Pain: They suspect money is leaking but can't prove it
- Motivation: Personal — if they find leakage, they look like a hero to the owner

## 2. Where To Find Them

### A. LinkedIn (Your strongest channel)
- Search: "Finance Manager" OR "CFO" OR "Managing Director" + "Lagos" OR "Abuja" OR "Port Harcourt"
- Filter: Companies with 50-500 employees
- Look for posts about cost-cutting, audits, or financial controls
- Connect with a soft message (template below), NOT a pitch

### B. Accounting Firms (Goldmine — they have multiple clients)
- Search Google for "accounting firms Lagos Nigeria" or "audit firms Abuja"
- Target: Small-to-mid audit firms (not Big 4 — they have their own tools)
- Pitch THEM: "I have a tool that can help your clients find leakage. You can offer it as a value-add service."
- Firms to target:
  - Search on NGEx.com (Nigerian business directory)
  - Check ICAN member directory (icanig.org) for registered firms
  - Look up companies on FIRS tax consultants list

### C. Chambers of Commerce & Business Associations
- Lagos Chamber of Commerce (LCCI) — lccinigeria.org
- Nigerian Association of Chambers of Commerce (NACCIMA)
- Manufacturers Association of Nigeria (MAN)
- These orgs have member directories you can access or request
- Attend their events — they're full of MDs and CEOs

### D. WhatsApp Business Groups
- Search for or ask to join: "Nigerian business owners" WhatsApp groups
- "Lagos entrepreneurs" WhatsApp groups
- "Nigerian finance professionals" groups
- Don't spam — offer value first ("I built a free tool that scans for financial leakage, anyone want to try it?")

### E. Cold Email (Using your Gmail)
- Find finance managers on LinkedIn, then guess their email:
  - firstname@companyname.com
  - firstname.lastname@companyname.com
  - Use hunter.io (free tier) to verify emails
- Send the outreach template below

### F. Referral Strategy (After your first pilot)
- Ask pilot customer: "Who else do you know that might have leakage?"
- Offer them 5% of recovery fees from any referral they send
- Finance people know other finance people — this compounds fast

## 4. Demo Script (15 minutes)

**Minute 0-2: Introduction**
"Thanks for your time. I built RevAI to solve a problem I kept seeing: companies losing money and not knowing where it's going. Let me show you how it works with your data."

**Minute 2-5: Upload + Scan**
- They go to https://myrevai.streamlit.app
- They enter company name and audit period
- They upload their AP and Payroll files
- They click "Run Full Scan"
- Wait for results (10-15 seconds)

**Minute 5-8: The Big Reveal**
- Point to the red banner: "This is your total financial leakage: NGN [X]"
- Let the number sink in. Don't talk for 5 seconds.
- Then: "Let me show you where this money is going."

**Minute 8-12: Walk Through Findings**
- Click Ghost Vendors tab — show vendor names that look suspicious
- Click Duplicates tab — show same invoice paid twice with dates
- Click VAT/WHT tab — show tax deducted but never remitted
- For each one say: "Does this vendor name look right to you?" or "Do you remember paying this invoice twice?"

**Minute 12-14: Export + Deliverables**
- Click "Download PDF Report" — they get a branded PDF
- Click "Download Excel" — they get all findings with vendor names, amounts, risk levels
- "You now have everything documented. You can take this to your team and start investigating."

**Minute 14-15: The Close**
"The scan found NGN [X] in potential leakage. I can help you build a recovery plan and walk your team through investigating each item. My fee is 10% of what we actually recover. If we don't recover anything, you pay nothing. Would you like to start?"

## 6. Common Objections & Responses

**"We already have an auditor."**
"That's great — RevAI isn't a replacement. It's a tool that helps your auditor work faster. They can scan your data in seconds instead of spending weeks manually checking. Would your auditor find this useful?"

**"My data is confidential."**
"Completely understood. Your data never leaves your browser. The scan runs locally — nothing is uploaded to any server. You can verify this in your browser's network tab during the demo."

**"How much does it cost?"**
"The scan is free. You only pay 10% of what we actually help you recover. If we find nothing, you pay nothing. So there's literally no risk."

**"I need to think about it."**
"Of course. The PDF and Excel reports are yours to keep either way. Take a look at the flagged items — if any of them turn out to be real leakage, let me know and we can discuss recovery."

**"We don't have that kind of leakage."**
"That's entirely possible — and if the scan confirms it, you'll have the documentation to prove your controls are working. Either way, you get value: you find leakage and recover it, or you get confirmation that your finances are clean."
